Get to know the things that make Pompón happiest. From his favorite meals and toys to his favorite places and activities, these are some of the little things that make his days special.

Things Pompón Loves

His Favorite Activities

Pompón enjoys simple activities that keep him entertained. He loves playing with his toys, watching birds through the window, exploring the house, and spending time with his family.

Because of his three-legged mobility, he has to be a little more careful than other cats, but that does not stop him from having fun.

His Favorite Spot

One of Pompón's favorite things to do is find a comfortable place near a window and watch what is happening outside.

Birds can quickly get his attention. He can stay focused on the window for a long time, quietly observing everything around him.
